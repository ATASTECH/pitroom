# Code of conduct

Pitroom is a small open-source project. We want it to be a place where people can ask, report and contribute without being worn down. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and every other space that belongs to the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be patient with questions, especially from newcomers, and assume good faith until shown otherwise.
- Keep it on topic and constructive: say what is wrong and, if you can, what would help.
- Respect other people's time and privacy: do not post someone's personal details, and remove secrets from logs before you share them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about a person's identity or background.
- Sexualized language or attention, stalking, or publishing private information.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, and deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban someone. They will try to be fair and to explain their decision, and they apply the same rules to themselves.

To report a problem, use the repository's private form (the **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**) and start the title with "Conduct:", or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile, [@ATASTECH](https://github.com/ATASTECH). Reports are handled privately and the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.

## Scope of these rules

This code of conduct covers behavior toward people in the project's spaces. Technical disagreements, reviews of code and saying no to a proposal are not violations.
